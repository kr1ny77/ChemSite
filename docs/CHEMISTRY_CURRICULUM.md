Target:

First-year university chemistry for 08.03.01 Construction / Civil Engineering.

## Source alignment (2026-09-29)

The public [MGSU Open Construction University chemistry course](https://osu.mgsu.ru/courses/ximiya) explicitly identifies 08.03.01 Construction and lists atomic structure and the periodic system, bonding and structure, thermodynamics, and kinetics and equilibrium. These align directly with ChemSite Modules 1, 2, 8, 9 and 10. The course page supplies a broad fallback reference; it does not expose enough detailed assessment criteria to certify the entire 200-task bank. The publicly indexed [MGSU 2025 chemistry work program for 08.03.01](https://isa.mgsu.ru/vikon/sveden/files/eiv/080301_PGS_B1_O_11_2025_brs.pdf) confirms a discipline document for the Construction track, but the source PDF was unavailable for detailed topic comparison during this review. A target UGNTU syllabus or an instructor review remains needed for course-specific fit.

The [UGNTU Architectural and Construction Institute program list](https://asf.ugntu.ru/page/bakalavriat-magistratura) confirms that the university offers 08.03.01 Construction. The public page lists study profiles and entry subjects; it supplies no detailed first-year chemistry outcomes. L2-050 was revised to an explicit molecular phosphorus-oxide equation using [PubChem's formula distinction](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Phosphorus-Pentoxide), so its answer no longer depends on the target course's empirical-formula convention.

## Expanded task bank — 2026-10-15

The combined content bank contains **1,000 fixed tasks**, comprising the original
200 and 800 additional records across **122 new learning families**. Parameterised
families deliberately revisit a skill with different substances or data. Counts
by game level: 200, 160, 240, 240, 160. The extension covers all thirteen existing
curriculum modules; this is a coverage map, with course-specific instructor
acceptance remaining a separate gate.

| Module | Additional tasks | Coverage examples |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 Atomic structure | 50 | Isotopes, ions, configurations, quantum numbers, Hund/Pauli, periodic trends |
| 2 Bonding | 10 | Ionic/covalent/metallic bonding, polarity, networks, hydrogen bonding |
| 3 Inorganic compounds | 80 | Charge-balanced formulas and unambiguous salt naming |
| 4 Oxidation states | 20 | Neutrality and conventional H/O assignments |
| 5 Reactions | 118 | Balancing, mole ratios, limiting reagent, conversion/yield, net ionic reasoning |
| 6 Solutions | 103 | Concentration, dilution, mixtures, crystallisation, molality, osmosis, ideal gas model |
| 7 Electrolytes | 97 | pH/pOH, buffers, hydrolysis, dissociation, Ksp/common ion, neutralisation |
| 8 Thermodynamics | 71 | Calorimetry, Hess, entropy, Gibbs energy and direction |
| 9 Kinetics | 32 | Rate data, order, rate laws, half-life, catalysis |
| 10 Equilibrium | 32 | K/Q, concentration exponents, dynamic equilibrium, pressure effects |
| 11 Redox | 22 | Electron equivalents, oxidant/reductant, iron and permanganate |
| 12 Electrochemistry | 45 | EMF, Faraday, Nernst, electrodes, salt bridge, galvanic corrosion |
| 13 Construction chemistry | 120 | Water balance/hardness, lime/gypsum, cement, carbonation, chlorides, polymers/silicates |

Each new record includes an objective, hint, explanation, rule and source links.
570 numeric records carry reproducible arithmetic evidence; 50 formula tasks
carry charge/atom evidence; 20 equation tasks conserve atoms. Forty distinct
transfer cases address interpretation and common misconceptions. All experimental
quantities are explicitly virtual or specified teaching models. Water-quality
limits in the cases are given educational criteria.

Recommended study order: Modules 1–4 → 5–7 → 8–12 → 13. Within a family, study
the rule, attempt a problem unaided, then explain a new case using that rule.
References include OpenStax Chemistry 2e, OpenStax Organic Chemistry §31.7,
USGS water hardness, American Cement Association, NIST durability publications
and USG gypsum chemistry. The MGSU fallback mapping above remains applicable;
full alignment to a particular UGNTU syllabus requires that syllabus and review.

## Detailed fallback program retrieved — 2026-10-15

An accessible official [MGSU chemistry work program for 08.03.01,
Technical Operation of Housing and Communal Facilities](https://mgsu.ru/vikon/sveden/files/eix/080301_TEOGKH_B1_O_11_2025_brs.pdf)
provides detailed outcomes (PDF pp. 2–3) and topic tables (pp. 4–6). It is the
2024-updated program, approved December 2024, for the 2023-entry profile; its
2025 filename does not change that identity. This supplies a detailed fallback
comparison. UGNTU-specific equivalence and instructor acceptance remain open.

The current 1000-task bank covers atomic/bonding/inorganic topics, stoichiometry,
solutions/electrolytes, thermodynamics, kinetics/equilibrium, redox/electrochemistry,
corrosion and selected construction/polymer properties. Inspection of actual
prompts/subtopics found missing dispersed/colloidal systems, sorption/wetting and
surfactants, named Arrhenius/Van't Hoff calculations, organic functional classes
and polymer-formation equations. Boiling-point elevation and quantitative gas-law
coverage also require explicit task evidence. Existing freezing-point, osmotic,
Nernst and polymer-property questions remain valid. Add these outcomes before
claiming complete fallback syllabus coverage. See docs/CURRICULUM_COVERAGE.md.
