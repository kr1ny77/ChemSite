## Source alignment (2026-09-29)

The public [MGSU Open Construction University chemistry course](https://osu.mgsu.ru/courses/ximiya) explicitly identifies 08.03.01 Construction and lists atomic structure and the periodic system, bonding and structure, thermodynamics, and kinetics and equilibrium. These align directly with ChemSite Modules 1, 2, 8, 9 and 10. The course page supplies a broad fallback reference; it does not expose enough detailed assessment criteria to certify the entire 200-task bank. The publicly indexed [MGSU 2025 chemistry work program for 08.03.01](https://isa.mgsu.ru/vikon/sveden/files/eiv/080301_PGS_B1_O_11_2025_brs.pdf) confirms a discipline document for the Construction track, but the source PDF was unavailable for detailed topic comparison during this review. A target UGNTU syllabus or an instructor review remains needed for course-specific fit.

The [UGNTU Architectural and Construction Institute program list](https://asf.ugntu.ru/page/bakalavriat-magistratura) confirms that the university offers 08.03.01 Construction. The public page lists study profiles and entry subjects; it supplies no detailed first-year chemistry outcomes.
