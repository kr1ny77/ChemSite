# ChemSite Game Design

## Vision

ChemSite should make difficult university chemistry feel like a real game.

The visual language uses:

- fixed isometric camera
- compact readable level
- small stylized character
- colorful construction environment
- stations around the map
- task cards at the top
- quick movement
- short interactions

The visual reference may take inspiration from readable cooperative cooking games while all characters, environments, assets, UI and animations remain original.

## Setting

A colorful stylized construction site.

Possible environment elements:

- unfinished building
- concrete columns
- reinforcement steel
- scaffolding
- crane
- pipes
- water tank
- cement bags
- pallets
- laboratory trailer
- concrete mixer
- testing equipment

## Player

One stylized construction chemistry student.

Design:

- large safety helmet
- reflective vest
- gloves
- boots
- expressive proportions
- clear silhouette from above

## Camera

Fixed isometric / top-down camera.

Requirements:

- entire relevant gameplay area visible
- player easy to track
- stations readable
- minimal camera movement
- optional subtle smoothing

## Core loop

1. A chemistry task appears.
2. Player determines the appropriate station.
3. Player runs to the station.
4. Player interacts.
5. A chemistry mini-game begins.
6. Player submits an answer.
7. Correct answer gives score and XP.
8. Incorrect answer gives a short explanation.
9. Weak concepts appear again later using a different question.
10. Complete the required tasks before the round ends.

## Level 1 rules

Time limit:
15 minutes.

Required tasks:
5.

The round ends immediately when all five tasks are completed.

Questions are selected from a much larger bank.

## Interaction styles

Avoid turning the game into a normal quiz website.

Use:

- formula assembly
- card matching
- classification
- ion selection
- equation balancing
- drag/select mechanics
- virtual reagent mixing
- virtual weighing
- pH analysis
- numerical calculations
- construction inspection
- multi-stage missions

## Progression

Five levels.

Each completed level awards:

- score
- XP
- 1 to 3 stars
- construction progress

Subsequent levels unlock progressively.

## Learning system

Track mastery by concept.

Example:

Nomenclature: 84%
Oxidation states: 62%
Ionic equations: 48%
Corrosion: 71%

Incorrect answers increase the probability of future questions about the same concept.

The next question should use different substances.

Example:

Mistake:
BaSO3

Later:
Na2SO3
CaSO3
Al2(SO4)3

## Scoring

Suggested:

Correct:
+100

3 consecutive correct:
x1.5 combo

5 consecutive correct:
x2

Incorrect:
combo reset

Fast answer:
time bonus

Hint:
small score reduction

## Feedback

Correct:

- short celebration
- station effect
- particles
- sound
- score animation

Incorrect:

- concise explanation
- correct answer
- relevant rule
- one small example

Avoid long interruptions.

## Construction progression

Success should visually improve the construction site.

Examples:

- new floor completed
- crane moves materials
- walls appear
- equipment activates
- scaffolding advances

The player should feel that chemistry knowledge is helping construct the building.

## Practice mode

The user chooses one topic and trains without career progression.

Practice mode may optionally disable the timer.

## Accessibility

Provide:

- readable fonts
- strong interaction indicators
- keyboard-only operation
- adjustable sound
- adjustable camera shake/effects
- optional reduced motion
