# ChemSite art direction

The site is a warm industrial diorama seen from an elevated camera. Safety amber marks task destinations; deep blue steel frames machinery; warm concrete and timber keep the play route legible. Chemistry cyan is reserved for equipment and player details.

Use softened edges, readable silhouettes, deliberate material families, and compact clusters around a clear traversal path. Hero assets receive Blender source, multiview review, and GLB validation. Kenney Factory and Building Kit models provide the initial modular foundation; production passes should refine them with coherent PBR surfaces, UVs, and scale checks.

The player reads as a young site chemist through an oversized helmet, safety vest, gloves, boots, and cyan chemistry pack. From the gameplay camera, the helmet and vest must remain distinct at normal zoom. Native Control UI uses large Russian labels and concise work orders.
