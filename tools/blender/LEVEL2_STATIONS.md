# Level 2 station modeling contract and reference notes

The four assets are desktop-game stations for a fixed isometric camera. Each occupies roughly a 2.3 × 1.3 m footprint and shares the site's concrete footing, teal powder-coated steel, amber safety trim, and cyan instrument accents. Authored parts remain editable in `tools/blender/build_level2_stations.py` and the four `.blend` files. Each station exports as a separate GLB with one simple collision proxy provided by Godot.

- Reaction bench: rear instrument display, supported sample vessels, capped containers, enclosure rail, and controls. The visual language follows the existing site laboratory and sealed classroom apparatus.
- Mixing station: a supported motor beam, two drive shafts, paddles, beakers, and a control readout. The relationship of motor beam → shafts → paddles → vessels follows [six-unit jar tester product photography](https://www.gogenlab.com/phipps-and-bird-7790-900-pb-900-6-paddle-programmable-jar-tester-with-1-liter-round-glass-beakers-s5795-1/). The real instrument uses more positions; two read more clearly at gameplay scale.
- Ionic station: a meter-style display, two electrodes descending into a vessel, and sample controls. The probe/vessel relationship follows [bench conductivity meter product photography](https://labstuff.eu/en/wtw-inolab-cond-720-conductivity-meter-with-tetracon325-probe.html). The two large discs are abstract ion glyphs for gameplay readability.
- Inspection station: raised checklist, sample holder, and a compact meter. This combines the existing construction material language with the meter and sample relationships in the conductivity reference.

Materials are authored colors without redistributed reference photography or textures. The model goal is polished stylized geometry with readable silhouettes, beveled manufactured edges, stable supports, and a shared palette. Review covers front, back, sides, top, perspective, and the 1440 × 900 gameplay camera. Fresh GLB import and Blender Agent Studio inspection report 5,088–6,336 triangles per station, 7–9 materials, zero invalid vertices, and no geometry issues. The four six-view sheets are in ignored `artifacts/*_views/`.
