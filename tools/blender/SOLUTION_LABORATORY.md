# Solution laboratory asset contract and reference notes

Purpose: a Level 3 station for molar mass, solution concentration, dilution, pH and virtual measurement tasks, readable from ChemSite's fixed isometric camera. The 2.65 × 1.55 m footprint carries a supported analytical balance with a draft shield on the left, volumetric vessels on the right, and a rear pH readout with a supported probe. Its steel, concrete, amber, cyan and frosted-glass palette matches the authored Level 1–2 stations. The station is virtual game equipment; the asset contains no practical preparation sequence.

Reference observations: [A&D balance product image](https://www.aandd.co.jp/products/weighing/balance/bal-analytical/bm/) shows the pan inside a transparent shield above a digital base. [A construction-material laboratory photo](https://www.aqualab.co.in/construction-material-testing.php) groups bottles, a meter and a balance on one bench. These relationships informed the left-to-right equipment grouping. The real shield and glassware are simplified into solid, beveled forms that remain legible at gameplay distance. No source photos or textures are redistributed.

Deliverables are the deterministic `build_solution_laboratory.py`, editable `source/solution_laboratory.blend`, and game-ready `assets/models/stations/solution_laboratory.glb`. The reviewed GLB measures 2.65 × 1.55 × 2.515 m, has 43 mesh objects, nine material families and 10,628 triangles. Blender Agent Studio fresh import reported no invalid vertices, degenerate faces or zero-length edges. Six-view evidence is in ignored `artifacts/solution_laboratory_views/`; the station was also inspected in the 1440 × 900 Godot site render. Godot supplies one simple box collision proxy.
