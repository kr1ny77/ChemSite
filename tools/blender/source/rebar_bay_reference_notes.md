# Rebar bay modeling notes

The project asset uses original geometry and materials. Construction references informed the vertical rod and transverse-tie arrangement; no external image or texture is embedded.

| Reference | Observed relationship | Applied interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kato Kensetsu reinforcement work](https://www.kato-kensetsu.co.jp/sekoujissekikako/freepage_106_1.html) | Vertical steel cages rise from a poured slab while formwork sits nearby. | Two starter cages on a concrete footing with one rear panel and one stored panel. |
| [Reinforcement cage example](https://sprut-ukraina.com.ua/articles/mehanicheskoe-soedinenie-armatury-kak-alternativa-obscheprinyatym-metodam-stykovki.html) | Upright bars are held together by repeated horizontal ties. | Four upright bars and seven rectangular ties per cage. |

Bars are slightly thicker than scale so the cage remains legible from the fixed isometric camera. The two cages keep distinct silhouettes at normal gameplay zoom.
