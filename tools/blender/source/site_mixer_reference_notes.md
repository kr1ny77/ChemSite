# Site mixer modeling notes

The project asset uses original geometry and materials. External equipment images informed the working silhouette and mechanical relationships; no source images or labels are embedded.

| Reference | Observed relationship | Applied interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Deper portable mixer](https://deper.com.ar/categorias/mezcla-de-materiales/8) | A tilted orange drum rests on a small wheeled steel chassis. | Flared drum, paired wheels and low frame. |
| [Deper compact mixer](https://deper.com.ar/productos/hormigonera-compact-150/6) | The front opening, side pivot and motor housing read from three-quarter views. | Recessed mouth, side axle housing and protected motor box. |
| [Paramount Browns mixer](https://www.paramountbrowns.com.au/products/deluxe-electric-cement-mixer-3-5cuft-cmi40e/) | A handwheel and front landing support explain how the drum tilts. | Side handwheel and foot under the chassis. |

The asset is sized for the fixed isometric site camera and keeps the opening visible from above. The native collision proxy covers its frame and drum as a single solid obstacle.
