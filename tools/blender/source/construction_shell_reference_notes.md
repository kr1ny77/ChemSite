# Construction shell contract and references

Asset: open concrete-frame shell for the left 6.3 × 4.3 m site pad. The frame uses four supported posts, connected edge beams, a partial upper slab, temporary formwork and edge rails. The center and station route remain open. Godot receives a reproducible GLB plus editable Blender source; simple collision proxies stay in Godot.

Style: softened concrete and dark steel, warm timber formwork, restrained safety amber. Target view: 1440 × 900 elevated game camera; secondary checks: front, sides, back and top. Performance target: fewer than 20,000 triangles. Review questions: does the open frame read as an active unfinished building, do the rebar bay and player remain visible, and do posts and temporary rails connect plausibly?

References viewed 2026-09-24:

- [OPPBTP slab-edge reference](https://content.preventionbtp.fr/uploads/images/35/3511aee571ce4d4ef45fcef4b3b7619258accc63.jpg): frontal concrete frame. Observed long horizontal slab edges on vertical supports, open ground-level bays, and continuous temporary rails at the top. Photo scale and precise dimensions are uncertain.
- [BMMI airport hotel shell](https://bmmisrq.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/SFW_BMMI_AiportHotel_01-2.jpg): three-quarter view. Observed partial formwork zones beside cast concrete, narrow rails around open slab edges, and a clearly legible post-and-beam rhythm. Site-scale proportions are adapted to the compact game pad.

The photographs guide structural relationships and silhouettes. Geometry, colors and layout are original to ChemSite.
